              Auto-rotate paper to
              

                
                
                
              

            

          

          
Measure what the pen can actually reach — the bed is the whole machine here, and anything past it is clipped at plot time. The presets are only starting points: rename, resize or delete any of them.

          
          

          

            Straight (true) axis
            

              
              
            

          

          
How far this machine's two axes are out of square, measured with the calculator below, and which axis is the straight one the other is corrected against. Applied to plots. Leave the angle at 0 unless you've measured a skew.

          

            When correction would clip
            

              
              
            

          

          
Correcting a nonzero skew angle can push ink past the page edge. Clip keeps artwork at its declared size and lets that ink clip. Shrink to fit reserves room and scales artwork down so nothing clips.

          

            Work it out from a test square
            
Plot a square, then measure it corner to corner. Both diagonals equal means no skew and nothing to correct.
